## Supported CLEP equivalencies

The supported CLEP mappings use FIU's published score of 50 and include Introductory Sociology, Introductory Psychology, American Government, College Algebra, Calculus, College Composition, Principles of Microeconomics, and Principles of Macroeconomics.

FIU identifies CLEP Introductory Sociology as elective credit. CreditMap therefore accepts SYG 2000 but does not allocate it to a modeled UCC or Finance requirement.

## Duplicate and shared-course handling

FIU states that duplicate exam credit is not awarded. CreditMap suppresses a second input resolving to the same FIU course.

Some FIU courses can appear in both the UCC and Business pre-core. CreditMap may show the same canonical course as satisfying both requirement labels, but the progress calculation counts the course credits only once.

## Deliberate limits

CreditMap returns **Verification required** for:

- manually entered dual-enrollment transfer decisions;
- grade, GPA, and attempt-limit compliance;
- Humanities, Natural Science, lab, Student Choice, and writing-intensive placement outside the supported records;
- civic-literacy, global-learning, foreign-language, summer, residency, and first-year conditions;
- elective approval and catalog-year applicability;
- the comprehensive examination and other record-level graduation checks.

The FIU model is a planning estimate, not an FIU degree audit. Students must confirm results in the Panther Degree Audit and with an academic adviser.
