## Why the exam source is statewide

Florida law and the Articulation Coordinating Committee require public state colleges and universities to award at least the listed credit when a student earns the published score. Institutions may award more, but cannot require a higher score for the listed minimum.

CreditMap therefore models the guaranteed minimum only. Statewide placeholders are mapped to UCF's published local numbers, including:

## Conservative boundaries

CreditMap returns **Verification required** for:

- manually entered dual-enrollment and transfer courses;
- Humanities, Natural Sciences, and GEP selections not represented by a supported exam record;
- minimum grades, primary-core and major GPAs, attempt limits, residency, upper-division, summer, foreign-language, and catalog-year checks;
- elective eligibility, prerequisites, substitutions, department approval, and offering terms;
- credit-by-exam beyond the guaranteed statewide minimum or beyond the applicable 45-credit limit.

CLEP Introductory Sociology produces SYG 2000 credit but remains elective-only in the supported 2026-2027 UCF GEP model because the statewide table notes that SYG X000 was removed from the general-education core effective 2024-2025.

The UCF model is a planning estimate, not a UCF degree audit. Students must confirm results in their official UCF degree audit and with an academic success coach.
