# Verified UF Finance data slice

Checked: **August 9, 2026**

CreditMap's first reviewed academic-data slice covers the University of Florida Finance BSBA pathway and the supported UF AP/CLEP equivalencies used by the local application.

## Official sources

| Source | Records captured | URL |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UF Undergraduate Catalog — Finance BSBA | 120-credit degree, critical tracking, business core, major courses, restricted electives, professional communication, and career readiness | https://catalog.ufl.edu/UGRD/colleges-schools/UGBUS/FIN_BSBA/ |
| UF Undergraduate Catalog — Exam Credit | Supported AP/CLEP score bands, UF course equivalents, credit amounts, incoming-credit limits, and duplicate-credit precedence | https://catalog.ufl.edu/UGRD/academic-advising/exam-credit/exam-credit.pdf |
| UF Undergraduate Catalog — General Education | 36-credit structure, subject areas, and incoming-credit guidance | https://catalog.ufl.edu/UGRD/academic-programs/general-education/ |

The live pages did not state a catalog year when checked. CreditMap therefore stores **Live UF catalog; year not stated on page** instead of guessing a year. A student's matriculation-year catalog remains controlling.

## Automated records

CreditMap currently automates the supported equivalencies for:

- AP Calculus AB and BC;
- AP English Language and Composition;
- AP Macroeconomics and Microeconomics;
- AP Psychology;
- AP Statistics;
- AP United States Government and Politics;
- CLEP Calculus, College Algebra, and College Composition;
- CLEP American Government, Introductory Psychology, and Introductory Sociology;
- CLEP Principles of Macroeconomics and Microeconomics.

Score-dependent AP ranges are stored as separate records. For example, AP Calculus BC score 3 and scores 4–5 do not share one flattened result.

## Deliberately not automated

CreditMap returns **Verification required** for:

- manually entered dual-enrollment and transfer courses;
- UF Gen Ed category placement not represented by a supported exam record;
- Quest, International, and Civic Literacy overlap or assessment checks;
- minimum-grade, attempt-limit, GPA, residency, and catalog-year checks;
- restricted-elective eligibility and career-readiness phase completion.

UF states that acceptable dual-enrollment and transfer courses from Florida public institutions generally follow the Statewide Course Numbering System. CreditMap still does not accept a manually typed code as proof that a particular student's course is transferable.

## Mixed-data boundary

FIU, FSU, UCF, and USF pathways remain illustrative competition fixtures. All tuition values remain illustrative estimates. The interface labels this boundary on every planning page and on each comparison card.
